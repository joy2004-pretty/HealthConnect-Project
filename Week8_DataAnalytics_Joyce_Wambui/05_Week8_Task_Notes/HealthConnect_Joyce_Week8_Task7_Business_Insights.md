# HealthConnect Rwanda | Week 8 Task 7

**Joyce · Data Analytics · Priority business insights**

The clearest finding is that **HealthConnect has a baseline for recorded activity, but no baseline for missed appointments**. The other figures help management see which totals it can use today and which comparisons need better data.

| Priority insight | What the evidence shows | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Bookings describe workload; attendance is unmeasured.** | The extract contains 5,000 appointments, with 1,235–1,271 records per quarter. It also shows 510 patients with more than one record. There is no attendance outcome. | The counts support a review of recorded workload. They cannot show which clinics, times or patient groups have higher no-show rates, or whether reminders would help. |
| **2. Overall collections reconcile; facility rankings cover a small share.** | Payments of RWF 8,389,454,556 equal 90.12% of RWF 9,309,401,961 billed. Only RWF 694,551,320, or 8.28% of collections, links to appointments in the extract. | The overall billed-versus-collected comparison is useful. A facility ranking would describe only the linked portion. The RWF 919,947,405 outstanding balance does not establish losses or how much is overdue. |
| **3. The overall feedback score has far wider coverage than the site breakdown.** | The overall mean is 2.99/5 across 21,064 responses. The 1,756 responses linked to appointments are 8.34% of the total and average 2.93/5. | The overall score summarises the responses received. The linked average is close, but that does not establish that the small linked group represents each facility. Site comparisons need response counts and a coverage note. |
| **4. The choice of join can overstate or understate workload.** | The Week 7 controlled bed-detail join inflated the department appointment count from 5,000 to 50,000. Separately, an appointment-matched view retained only 2,272 of 5,000 laboratory tests. | A staffing or workload review could be distorted in either direction. The source total and the portion that can be attributed to a department should be reported separately. |

**Presentation emphasis:** Lead with the attendance gap, then use the department join test as a concrete example of why a chart's coverage matters. Keep the reported revenue summary (RWF 1,132,677,500) separate from payments received.

**Evidence:** Week 8 project guide, Data Analytics task 7 (p. 6); Joyce's Week 7 Testing and Refinement Report; the Week 8 KPI, dashboard and appointment checks; supplied appointment, billing, payment, laboratory and feedback extracts. RWF follows the Week 7 reporting assumption.
